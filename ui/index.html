Backbone Fidelity Lab

Keep fewer edges, keep the behaviour? Explore five sparsification methods on temporal contact networks (replication of Pereira, Costa & Rocha 2026).

The question

A contact network has many weak edges. A backbone keeps only the important ones. X is the adjusted size of the backbone: 0 = just a spanning tree, 1 = the full graph. Every method is compared at the same X, then scored on how well it preserves what the network does:
  1. ρC – rank correlation of eigenvector centrality (who is important)
  2. ρt – rank correlation of SI infection times (who gets infected when)
  3. ξ⁻¹ – ratio of half-infection times (how fast the epidemic spreads)
